# Homepage

Scope: index.html single-page homepage (src/App.tsx and src/components/*). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: women around Thiruvanmiyur, mostly on phones, choosing sarees, blouses, aari work or custom stitching. Action: enquire on WhatsApp or call; visit the boutique.
Constraints: real business details only; products, prices and reviews stay labelled as demo; stock photos are placeholders for the boutique's own.
Visual reference pinned by the user: shifraworld (polish, editorial rhythm, motion), with Mayuram's own identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Chennai saree-and-blouse atelier, not a mall storefront: real silk on real women, framed like the arched doorway of a boutique, with the aari needle's running stitch as the thread that ties the page together. Refuses procedural product art, pill-shaped SaaS cards and generic gradient blobs.

OWN-WORLD: Poster palette: midnight #16061B and plum #5A254E bands, gold #DBA01C hairlines and fills, one light ground, warm ivory #F7F3EA, for product and craft bands; peacock teal #0E5E66 as the single secondary (from the peacock in the logo), owning the testimonials band. Square-cornered photographs with an inset 1px gold frame; arch-topped hero and services frames. Gold dashed "stitch" lines. Brygada 1918 display (roman with italic accents; replaced Bodoni Moda for legibility), Manrope body; tracked uppercase only on controls and small captions, never as eyebrows above headings. Square buttons.

STORY: Visitor sees silk worn well, understands Mayuram sells sarees and makes blouses by hand, trusts the craft through close-ups and the made-to-measure process, then messages on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Midnight-to-plum field. Left column (~53%): two-line Brygada headline with gold italic second line (no eyebrow), one sentence, gold primary + outline secondary buttons. Right: arch-topped portrait frame (~470px wide) cross-fading three model photos, a dashed stitch arch sewing itself around it on every slide, a swaying swing-tag caption naming the piece. Named slide tabs (Sarees, Blouses, Bridal) with progress hairlines bottom left, arrows bottom right. Highlights strip below. Phone: arch photo first (46svh), headline and buttons beneath.

FORM: user-pinned reference direction (Shifra World's polish and motion, Mayuram's own identity); no concept roll ran, so there is no seed reference.

Signature interaction: the stitch. Gold dashed lines draw themselves in (hero arch per slide, the rule under each section heading, the four-step made-to-measure path, scroll-linked). Motion grammar: slow crossfade with gentle zoom, masked line rise for headlines, single fade-up reveals, image swap to a detail crop on product hover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
