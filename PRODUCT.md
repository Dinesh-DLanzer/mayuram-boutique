# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind 3, Framer Motion; static build deployed to GitHub Pages.

## Users

Women in and around Thiruvanmiyur, Chennai, browsing sarees, blouses, aari work and custom stitching, usually on a phone. They finish by calling or messaging on WhatsApp to enquire or book a stitching appointment.

## Purpose

Homepage for Mayuram Boutique (contact: Vijayalakshmi): stitching, aari work, clothes. Orders and enquiries hand off to WhatsApp; there is no payment, login or backend.

## Constraints

- Business details in src/config/site.ts come from the real visiting card; keep them.
- Product, price and testimonial content is demo content and stays labelled as such.
- Social links stay empty until confirmed.
- Imagery: real photographs (free-licence stock placeholders for now, credited), replacing the procedural renders.
